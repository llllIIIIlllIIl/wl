## the state od commercial plateforms

Spotify isn't giving any money to the artists for each tracks under 1k plays. WHICH MEANS THAT EVEN IF YOU PAY FOR PREMIUM OF YOU LISYEN TO INDIE/NICHE MUSIC NOTHING GOES TO THE THINGS YOU LISTEN TO AND THE MONEY ACTUALLY OES TO THE BIG MAJORS RELEASING ARTISTS THAT MAKE COMMERCIAL MUSIC WITH ALREADY MILLIONS OF STREAMS.
+the spotify owner invest money into war drones so it's up to you to decode what you wanna do with your money.

I personaly use the Deezer free trial each month and create a family in which I invite back my main account into each month. Then with the open source app Deemix I can even download anything in .flac

And when i need to listen to a spotify link someone sends me I open it in the web version of spotify on firefox with Ublock origin with all lists enabled. That way you don't have any ads in spotify but sound quality is max 128kbps because that's what the free plan provides.

If you really want a streaming plateforms the only fine option is Qobuz
